\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}

We position Project-Utopia along two axes: a tool-use taxonomy that locates
our \emph{multi-channel orchestration} regime (\S\ref{sec:rw-tool-use}), and
the RL evaluation methodology whose scoring ingredients we borrow
(\S\ref{sec:rw-rl-methodology}). A complete prior-art table for the 75+
benchmarks reviewed is in Appendix~\ref{app:prior-art-table}.

\subsection{LLM Tool-Use Spans Three Regimes}
\label{sec:rw-tool-use}

LLM tool-use benchmarks fall into three regimes distinguished by the
\emph{shape} of the action surface, not its size; the first two are saturated,
the third --- where Project-Utopia sits --- has none.

\paragraph{Single-channel selection.}
The dominant regime evaluates a single agent emitting tool invocations into
an open action set, scoring selection accuracy or single-turn invocation
success: \textbf{ReAct}~\citep{Yao2023ReAct} interleaved reasoning + acting;
\textbf{Toolformer}~\citep{Schick2023Toolformer} self-supervised invocation;
\textbf{Gorilla}~\citep{Patil2023Gorilla} and
\textbf{ToolLLM}~\citep{Qin2024ToolLLM} scaling to $10^3$--$10^4$ APIs scored
by selection accuracy; \textbf{MINT}~\citep{Wang2024MINT} multi-turn user
feedback; \textbf{HuggingGPT}~\citep{Shen2023HuggingGPT} model-zoo dispatch.
The single-agent web/task-bench literature --- AgentBench~\citep{Liu2024AgentBench},
GAIA~\citep{Mialon2023GAIA}, WebArena~\citep{Zhou2024WebArena},
AgentBoard~\citep{Ma2024AgentBoard}, BALROG~\citep{Paglieri2025BALROG},
TheAgentCompany~\citep{Xu2025TheAgentCompany},
OdysseyBench~\citep{Wang2025OdysseyBench}, HeroBench~\citep{Anokhin2025HeroBench}
--- shares the same shape: one agent, one (possibly large) action set, success
per episode.

\paragraph{Multi-agent (multi-LLM).}
The second regime instantiates \emph{multiple} agents, each with its own
action space, and studies their coordination protocol:
\textbf{ChatDev}~\citep{Qian2024ChatDev} (chat-chain of SWE roles),
\textbf{MetaGPT}~\citep{Hong2024MetaGPT} (publish-subscribe pool),
\textbf{AgentVerse}~\citep{Chen2024AgentVerse} (4-stage recruitment),
\textbf{CAMEL}~\citep{Li2023CAMEL} (2-agent role-playing), and
\textbf{Concordia}~\citep{Vezhnevets2023Concordia} (Game-Master broker);
\textbf{X-MAS}~\citep{Ma2025XMAS} and Anthropic's multi-agent
system~\citep{AnthropicMultiAgent2025} systematize heterogeneous-LLM routing
across 27 LLMs and intra-family production deployments. The regime studies
\emph{between-agent} coordination; each agent owns its own action surface.

\paragraph{Multi-channel orchestration of a fixed tool surface (our regime).}
We isolate a third regime in which \emph{one} agent must coherently
orchestrate \emph{multiple parallel decision channels} into a \emph{fixed}
schema-validated tool surface, at distinct cadences and under stale
long-horizon context. The closest neighbours sit in adjacent regimes:
\textbf{Voyager}~\citep{Wang2023Voyager} is depth-first in one Minecraft
domain via skill discovery (one channel, open action set), and
\textbf{Orak}~\citep{Krafton2026Orak} evaluates LLMs on 12 games via MCP
(one channel per game). No existing benchmark makes \emph{cross-channel
orchestration coherence} the unit of measurement. Project-Utopia fixes the
tool surface (4 tools $\times$ 4 channels) and reports per-channel,
per-dimension scores that single-channel benchmarks cannot produce.

\subsection{RL Evaluation Methodology (Borrowed)}
\label{sec:rw-rl-methodology}

Although Project-Utopia is not an RL benchmark, the metric stack borrows
four ingredients from RL game-playing AI: Crafter~\citep{Hafner2022Crafter}'s
geometric-mean composite (punishes single-axis collapse);
MeltingPot~2.0~\citep{Agapiou2022MeltingPot}'s sandwich normalization
$(\text{focal}-\text{random})/(\text{exploiter}-\text{random})$ for cross-cell
anchoring; Pluribus AIVAT~\citep{Brown2019Pluribus}'s PolicyValue Baseline for
variance reduction; and HELM's Mean Win Rate~\citep{Liang2023HELM} for
cross-cell aggregation.
