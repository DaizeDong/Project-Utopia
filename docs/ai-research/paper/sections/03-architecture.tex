\section{Project-Utopia Architecture}
\label{sec:architecture}

Project-Utopia is the elicitation substrate that makes stale coherence
measurable. To dissociate verbal mention from sustained behavioral
commitment we need (i) continuous structured-output emission across
multiple temporal scales, (ii) a deterministic environment whose
state-references can be machine-checked against the LLM's directives,
and (iii) an anchor injection protocol that pairs verbal tokens with
implicit action goals. We satisfy these three requirements with a
$4\,\text{channel}\,\times\,4\,\text{tool}$ substrate over a seeded,
bit-identically reproducible simulator. The substrate is the
\emph{elicitation method}, not the contribution of the paper; the
contribution is the dual-probe characterization of stale coherence
that it makes possible (\S\ref{sec:metrics}).

\subsection{Multi-Cadence Substrate}
\label{sec:channels}

The substrate emits structured output across three nested temporal
scales through four parallel LLM decision channels, each bound to one
deterministic tool. The cadence spread is what dissociates verbal
mention from behavioral commitment: a single-cadence harness cannot
tell whether an LLM continues to \emph{summarize} an anchor after it
has stopped \emph{acting} on it.

\paragraph{Environment-director} (cadence: 5--15\,s; tool: event
scheduler). Schema: \{weather: enum \texttt{clear|rain|storm},
\texttt{durationSec} $\in$ [8, 180], \texttt{factionTension} $\in$
[0, 1], \texttt{eventSpawns}: $\leq$3 with intensity $\geq 0.4$\}.
Coarse-cadence state perturbations to which the policy channel must
adapt, matching the ReAct~\citep{Yao2023ReAct} observe$\to$act loop.

\paragraph{NPC-policy} (cadence: 5--15\,s; tools: A* path query,
Boids steering, FSM intent applier). Schema: $\{\text{groupId},
\text{intentWeights} \in [0,3]^{|\mathcal{I}_g|},
\text{riskTolerance} \in [0,1],
\text{targetPriorities} \in [0,3]^{|\mathcal{T}_g|},
\text{ttlSec} \in [8, 24\,\text{h}]\}$. Intent weights flow through
\texttt{POLICY\_INTENT\_TO\_STATE} into per-NPC FSM preferences;
\texttt{targetPriorities} drive A* target selection; group Boids
steering consumes \texttt{intentWeights}. The
\texttt{targetPriorities} and \texttt{intentWeights} channels carry
the \emph{action-token sets} that the action-grounded recall probe
(\S\ref{sec:dualprobe}) machine-checks against injected anchors.

\paragraph{Strategic-plan} (cadence: 90\,s heartbeat + crisis
triggers; tool: plan arbitration). Schema:
\{\texttt{primaryGoal}: str, \texttt{constraints}: str[],
\texttt{resourceBudget}: \{food, wood, stone\}\}. The longest-cadence
channel carries the \emph{verbal summaries} in
\texttt{primaryGoal}/\texttt{constraints} that the verbal-recall
probe scans for anchor token mentions.

\paragraph{Colony-agent} (cadence: sim-time-gated + plan-stall
triggers; tool: build planner over PlanExecutor). Schema:
$[(\text{action: enum}, \text{args: obj}, \text{depends\_on:
int[]})]$. Wraps a Perceive $\to$ Plan $\to$ Ground $\to$ Execute
$\to$ Evaluate $\to$ Reflect loop~\citep{Yao2023ReAct,
Shinn2023Reflexion} that grounds multi-step build plans
tile-by-tile.

\subsection{Anchor Injection Protocol}
\label{sec:anchors}

At $t=0$ we inject \textbf{5 anchor entries} into the LLM's working
memory via the system prompt's \texttt{world\_summary} field. Each
anchor carries two paired fields:

\begin{lstlisting}[language=Python, caption={Anchor entry shape}]
{
  "verbal_tokens": ["warehouse at (12,8)"],
  "implicit_goals": [
      {"action": "deliver", "target": "warehouse"}
  ]
}
\end{lstlisting}

The two fields are deliberately \emph{not} co-presented as an
instruction (``you must deliver to (12,8)''). The anchor appears as
observed world state. The LLM must autonomously integrate it into
its directive stream: verbal tokens may resurface in
\texttt{primaryGoal}/\texttt{strategy.notes}, and implicit goals may
be served by allocating non-trivial weight to
\texttt{\{action:deliver, target:warehouse\}} in the
\texttt{targetPriorities}/\texttt{intentWeights} channels. The two
paths are independent: an LLM may surface verbal tokens without
serving implicit goals (stale coherence), serve implicit goals
without surfacing verbal tokens (silent commitment), or do both
(coherent) or neither (forgotten).

The 5-anchor count is empirically motivated. In pilot runs
(Appendix~\ref{app:b-additional}) the verbal-recall probe saturates
near 1.0 with $<5$ anchors (capacity not stressed) and the
instruction-following probe collapses for the smaller open-weight
models with $>5$ anchors (capacity exceeded); $n=5$ is the elbow at
which both probes have measurable variance across our model menu.

\subsection{Schema Validation and Guardrails}
\label{sec:contract}

The substrate must distinguish reasoning drift from parse error.
Two filters sit between every LLM emission and the tool layer.
\textbf{ResponseSchema} performs JSON-Schema validation (unknown
enums dropped, NaN rejected, types coerced). \textbf{Guardrails}
clamps numeric ranges ($\text{weights} \in [0,3]$,
$\text{risk} \in [0,1]$, $\text{TTL} \in [8\,\text{h}, 24\,\text{h}]$)
and is an idempotent fixed point:
$\texttt{guard(guard(x)) == guard(x)}$ for every directive shape
(generalizing the typed-function-signature pattern of
ToolLLM~\citep{Qin2024ToolLLM} and Gorilla~\citep{Patil2023Gorilla}
to four parallel channels). Idempotency matters here because
clamped output from one channel re-enters another channel's prompt
on the next tick; a non-idempotent guard would compound rather than
stabilize. In the stale-coherence context the schema layer is
\emph{plumbing, not the headline}: its role is to provide
machine-checkable ground truth for whether a directive ``references
an anchor'' or not, so that the dual probes
(\S\ref{sec:dualprobe}) measure reasoning behavior rather than JSON
malformedness.

\subsection{Determinism Seam}
\label{sec:adapter-seam}

Without bit-identical reproducibility we cannot distinguish stale
coherence from hardware variance. RNG flows through
\texttt{project\_utopia.app.rng}; A* paths are cached by
$(\text{faction}, \text{gridVersion}, \text{costVersion})$; Boids
accumulators run in fixed order; \texttt{PathWorkerPool} is disabled
in \texttt{deterministic=True} mode. \texttt{project\_utopia.tools.audit.determinism\_check}
runs the harness twice on the same seed and compares SHA-256 hashes
of slim state snapshots; three tiers pass with bit-identical hashes:
30 ticks \texttt{be19781c\ldots}, 1\,800 ticks
\texttt{f7a099a4\ldots}, 7\,200 ticks \texttt{43cb6aec\ldots}
(Appendix~\ref{app:determinism-report}).

The \textbf{AgentAdapter} is the interface any policy (LLM,
fallback, scripted oracle) implements to drive the four channels:

\begin{lstlisting}[language=Python, caption={AgentAdapter contract}]
async def request(self, channel: str, payload: Any,
                  options: dict | None = None) -> DecisionResponse:
    # channel in {environment-director, npc-policy,
    #             strategic-plan, colony-agent}
    # MUST NOT raise; on failure return a fallback DecisionResponse
    ...
\end{lstlisting}

Concrete adapters: \texttt{NoopAgentAdapter} (sandwich-norm lower
bound), \texttt{LLMClient} (OpenAI-compatible HTTP via litellm),
\texttt{FlatBaselineAdapter} (single-channel control that emits
per-worker FSM-state preferences directly), and
\texttt{ScriptedOraclePolicy} (hand-tuned upper bound). Tier-2
deterministic-inference adapters and cassette replay are documented
in \S\ref{sec:reproducibility}. The per-channel table mapping each
directive schema to its tool implementation is deferred to
Appendix~\ref{app:b-additional}; it is supporting machinery for the
dual probe, not the central object of study.
