\section{Metric Stack}
\label{sec:metrics}

The methodological centerpiece of this paper is the
\textbf{MemoryDegradation dual probe}: a pair of independent probes
that, taken jointly with a behavioral-drift discount, operationally
define stale coherence. Everything else in this section is supporting
machinery for the dual probe.

\subsection{The Dual Probe}
\label{sec:dualprobe}

Let $\mathcal{A} = \{a_1, \ldots, a_5\}$ denote the five anchors
injected at $t=0$ (\S\ref{sec:anchors}). Each anchor $a_i$ carries a
verbal-token set $V_i$ and an implicit-goal action-token set $G_i$.
At every sample point $t > 0$ we tap two disjoint outputs of the
substrate.

\paragraph{Verbal probe: \texttt{anchored\_fact\_recall}.}
$$
\text{verbal\_recall}(t) \;=\; \frac{1}{|\mathcal{A}|}
\sum_{i=1}^{|\mathcal{A}|}
\mathbf{1}\!\bigl[\,V_i \subseteq \mathrm{tokens}\bigl(\,
\texttt{world\_summary}(t)\,\cup\,
\texttt{strategy.notes}(t)\,\bigr)\,\bigr]
$$
the fraction of injected anchors whose verbal tokens still appear in
the LLM-emitted long-cadence summary channel
(\texttt{primaryGoal}/\texttt{strategy.notes} of the strategic-plan
channel). Range $[0, 1]$.

\paragraph{Action-grounded probe: \texttt{action\_grounded\_recall}.}
$$
\text{action\_recall}(t) \;=\; \frac{1}{|\mathcal{A}|}
\sum_{i=1}^{|\mathcal{A}|}
\mathbf{1}\!\bigl[\,\exists\,g \in G_i :
w_g(t) \ge \tau\,\bigr]
$$
the fraction of injected anchors whose implicit-goal action-tokens
are still served with non-trivial weight $w_g(t) \ge \tau$ in the
LLM-emitted \texttt{targetPriorities} / \texttt{intentWeights}
channels. We fix $\tau = 0.3$ on the clamped $[0, 3]$ weight scale
(pre-registered; sensitivity to $\tau \in \{0.1, 0.5\}$ reported in
Appendix~\ref{app:b-additional}). Range $[0, 1]$.

\paragraph{Dissociation metric.} Stale coherence is operationalized
as the difference of the two probes:
$$
\Delta(t) \;=\; \text{verbal\_recall}(t) \;-\; \text{action\_recall}(t).
$$
$\Delta(t) > 0$ is the \emph{stale-coherence regime} (says it,
doesn't act on it). $\Delta(t) \approx 0$ is coherent recall.
$\Delta(t) < 0$ is silent commitment (acts without summarizing), an
unusual regime we report for completeness but do not target.

\paragraph{Behavioral drift discount: \texttt{behavioral\_drift}.}
$$
\text{drift}(t) \;=\; 1 \;-\;
\frac{|\,\mathcal{D}(0) \,\cap\, \mathcal{D}(t)\,|}
     {|\,\mathcal{D}(0) \,\cup\, \mathcal{D}(t)\,|}
$$
where $\mathcal{D}(t)$ is the action-token set emitted at time $t$
across the \texttt{targetPriorities} / \texttt{intentWeights}
channels (Jaccard distance between $t=0$ and $t$). Drift
disentangles two confounded explanations of high $\Delta$: (a) the
LLM \emph{switched goals} (high drift, $\Delta$ explained by goal
displacement, \emph{not} stale coherence); (b) the LLM
\emph{stopped acting on this goal while still describing it} (low
drift, high $\Delta$, \emph{stale coherence}).

\paragraph{Identification.} Stale coherence is the joint regime
\begin{equation}
\text{verbal\_recall}(t) \;\text{high},\quad
\text{action\_recall}(t) \;\text{low},\quad
\text{drift}(t) \;\text{low}.
\label{eq:stalecoh-id}
\end{equation}
The three signals together identify the phenomenon. The dual probe
alone (without drift) cannot distinguish stale coherence from goal
displacement; drift alone (without the two probes) cannot identify
the verbal--action gap.

\subsection{Pre-Registered Statistical Tests}
\label{sec:tests}

We pre-register three tests on the dual probe, evaluated on the E1
matrix of \S\ref{sec:experiments}.

\paragraph{T1 --- Per-family existence test.} For each LLM family
$m$, one-sided Welch $t$-test of $\Delta(t = 4\,\text{h}) > 0.3$,
significance level $\alpha = 0.05$. We report effect size,
$t$-statistic, and the 95\% Bayesian credible interval (Beta-Binomial,
below) on the per-cell mean $\Delta$. Acceptance of $H$ for family
$m$ requires both $p < 0.05$ \emph{and} the credible interval lower
bound strictly above 0.

\paragraph{T2 --- Independence from raw recall.} Spearman rank
correlation
$\rho\bigl(\Delta(t=4\,\text{h}),\;\text{verbal\_recall}(t=4\,\text{h})\bigr)$
across $(\text{model}, \text{seed}, \text{scenario})$ cells, with
the LongMemEval~\citep{Wu2025LongMemEval}/RULER~\citep{Hsieh2024RULER}
recall-proxy as the verbal-recall baseline. Pre-registered target:
$\rho \in [-0.5, +0.5]$. If $|\rho| > 0.5$, stale coherence reduces
to a known long-context recall bias and the phenomenon does not
warrant a new axis.

\paragraph{T3 --- Cross-family heterogeneity.} Do the
stale-coherence onset times $t^\star(m)$ cluster across families, or
do families have distinct onset profiles? Kruskal--Wallis on
$\{t^\star(m) : m \in \text{model menu}\}$ followed by pairwise
Mann--Whitney with Bonferroni correction. Distinct onset profiles
provide camera-ready evidence that stale coherence is a per-family
characterization, not a universal context-length artifact.

\subsection{Supporting Metrics}
\label{sec:supporting}

The remaining 15 dimension keys
(\texttt{rae\_*}, \texttt{intent\_entropy},
\texttt{coalition\_coupling}, \texttt{state\_target\_obedience},
\texttt{faction\_responsiveness}, \texttt{dte\_per\_completion\_token},
\texttt{dte\_per\_decision}, \texttt{first\_token\_latency\_p50},
\texttt{plan\_policy\_alignment},
\texttt{env\_threat\_responsiveness},
\texttt{colony\_cadence\_health}) emitted by the five dimension
plugins (\texttt{project\_utopia/benchmark/dimensions/}) provide
context about \emph{how} stale coherence manifests in resource
allocation and group dynamics, but are not used as evidence for
hypothesis $\mathbf{H}$. They are reported in
Appendix~\ref{app:b-additional}.

\paragraph{Aggregation pipeline.} Across cells we use the standard
cross-cell normalization-and-aggregation pipeline:
\textbf{DimensionNormalizer} maps each plugin key to $[0, 1]$
benefit form via one of six transforms (\texttt{identity},
\texttt{invert}, \texttt{reciprocal}, \texttt{clipScale},
\texttt{rescaleSymmetric}, \texttt{exponentialDecay}); a reflective
unit test enforces one-and-only-one transform registration per key.
\textbf{Sandwich normalization}~\citep{Agapiou2022MeltingPot} pairs
each normalized score with a fallback (\texttt{Guardrails}-only) and
oracle (\texttt{ScriptedOraclePolicy}) reference run on the same
seed/scenario and reports
$S = (S_{\text{LLM}} - S_{\text{fallback}}) /
(S_{\text{oracle}} - S_{\text{fallback}})$ without clipping the
upper bound. The Crafter geometric mean~\citep{Hafner2022Crafter}
collapses per-resource sufficiencies to \texttt{rae\_composite}.
\textbf{Bayesian Beta-Binomial} posteriors with Beta(2, 2) prior
provide credible intervals at the $N=5$-seed budget where
central-limit-theorem Wald intervals
under-cover~\citep{Bowyer2025DontUseClt}; a prior-sensitivity sweep
over Jeffreys (0.5, 0.5) and uniform (1, 1) is reported in
Appendix~\ref{app:prior-sensitivity}. A Pluribus
AIVAT-style~\citep{Brown2019Pluribus} PolicyValue Baseline (PVB)
correction reduces variance by $\sim$2--3$\times$ on E1 pilot logs.

\paragraph{Cross-model leaderboard.} For the supporting
cross-model leaderboard we follow HELM Mean Win
Rate~\citep{Liang2023HELM}; the full MWR construction and
$\alpha$-rank~\citep{Lanctot2019OpenSpiel} fallback for
non-transitive payoff matrices are deferred to
Appendix~\ref{app:b-additional}. MWR does not measure stale
coherence and is not used to test $\mathbf{H}$; it is a
camera-ready convenience for reader comparison across LLM families.
