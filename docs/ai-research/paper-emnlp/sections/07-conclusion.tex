\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduce a dual probe for verbal--action recall in
structured-output LLM agents, a deterministic substrate that
makes the probe measurable, and a pilot across three model
families showing existence at threshold magnitude, tier
stratification, and horizon-dependent direction. An external
LongMemEval-style probe at matched context lengths recalls every
anchor perfectly, distinguishing the dual probe from generic
verbal-recall failure. We recommend co-deploying an
action-grounded probe with verbal-only memory health checks for
long-horizon production agents.
