\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduce a dual probe for verbal--action recall in
structured-output LLM agents, a deterministic substrate that
wires the action channel into observable worker behaviour, and a
pilot of DeepSeek-v4-Flash across three scenarios showing
dissociation that reaches threshold magnitude in two of eighteen
cells, separates by scenario at the short horizon, and attenuates
roughly $2.5\times$ at the long horizon. A pre-registered external
LongMemEval-style probe on three model families at matched
context lengths recalls every recovered anchor perfectly,
distinguishing the substrate dissociation from generic
verbal-recall failure. We frame the result as a substrate-coupled
single-family pilot; the cross-family extension and the
cross-substrate validation are the gating open questions.
