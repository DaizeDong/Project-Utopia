\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduce a dual probe for verbal--action recall in
structured-output LLM agents, a deterministic substrate that
wires the action channel into observable worker behaviour, and a
pilot across three model families showing dissociation that
reaches threshold magnitude, stratifies by tier, and reverses
direction with horizon. An external LongMemEval-style probe at
matched context lengths recalls every anchor perfectly,
distinguishing the substrate dissociation from generic
verbal-recall failure. We frame the result as a substrate-coupled
pilot; cross-substrate validation is the gating open question.
