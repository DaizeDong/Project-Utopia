\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The eighteen-cell pilot is narrower than the matrix needed to
characterise the dual probe definitively: three seeds per
scenario at two horizons on a single model family cannot support
strong claims about the scenario-stratified short-horizon
ordering, the horizon-attenuation pattern, or the independence of
dissociation magnitude from external verbal recall. We report the
pilot as a threshold-crossing existence and scenario-stratification
result, and the H2 independence finding as \emph{supported on the
pre-registered three-family probe} rather than as a general
property of the dual probe. The pre-registered wider design
(three model families, three scenarios, five seeds, four
horizons) is the subset we could run under the per-model API
availability we had during the study window; the cross-family
extension and the full pre-registered matrix are the immediate
experimental priorities for follow-on work.

A specific operational limitation: the external verbal-recall
probe recovered sixteen of eighteen cells with real-LLM responses;
two Claude-Sonnet-4.6 60-minute cells failed when the proxy
wallet was exhausted by the ${\sim}25{,}000$-token prompts. The
methodological circularity of the earlier AFR-as-its-own-proxy
formulation is resolved by the external probe, but the perfect-
recall ceiling effect on the recovered cells is itself a soft
limit: a harder probe (more anchors, harder distractors, longer
context) might puncture the ceiling and produce a measurable
non-zero correlation. We frame the H2 outcome as independence
supported \emph{by the absence of observable coupling at the
LongMemEval-style probe's difficulty level}, and identify a
harder external probe as the next experimental step.

A more fundamental concern is that the dual probe operates inside
substrates we designed to host it. The independent resource
dynamics (Section~\ref{sec:method:substrate}) rule out the most
degenerate failure mode---AGR collapsing into a JSON-token echo
on the colony substrate---and the cross-substrate replication on
a minimal dialogue agent loop (Section~\ref{sec:pilot:h3}) rules
out the next-most-degenerate reading, that the dissociation is
colony-specific. But the dialogue substrate is also one we
authored, and two substrates are not a population; the strongest
remaining validation would re-instantiate the dual probe on an
existing structured-output agent benchmark instrumented post-hoc.
We identify that as the gating priority for follow-on work
alongside the broader matrix sweep, and read the present findings
as conditional on it.
