\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The eighteen-cell pilot we report is narrower than the matrix that
would be required to characterise the dual probe definitively.
Three seeds per family on a single scenario at two horizons cannot
support strong claims about the tier ordering we observe, the
horizon-dependent reversal we observe in two of three families, or
the independence of dissociation magnitude from external verbal
recall. We report the pilot as an existence and tier-stratification
result, and we report the H2 independence finding as
\emph{supported on this pilot} rather than as a general property of
the dual probe; both characterisations require a broader matrix to
elevate them to generalisable claims.

We pre-registered a wider design than the one this pilot reports.
The matrix as executed is the subset of the pre-registered design
that completed under the rate budget available to us during the
study window. The narrowing is a constraint, not a methodological
choice: we ran the pilot we could run and report its results
honestly. The full pre-registered matrix is the immediate
experimental priority for follow-on work.

A specific operational limitation tied to the H2 test deserves
mention. The external verbal-recall probe recovered sixteen of
eighteen cells with real-LLM responses; two Claude-Sonnet-4.6
60-minute cells failed when the model-proxy wallet was exhausted
mid-run by the ${\sim}25{,}000$-token prompts and could not be
recovered within the study window. The methodological circularity
of the earlier AFR-as-its-own-proxy formulation is resolved by the
external probe, but the perfect-recall ceiling effect we observe
on the recovered cells is itself a soft limit: a harder probe (more
anchors, harder distractors, a longer context) might puncture the
ceiling and produce a measurable non-zero correlation. We frame
the H2 outcome as independence supported \emph{by the absence of an
observable coupling at the difficulty level the LongMemEval-style
probe sets}, and we identify a harder external probe as the next
experimental step.

A more fundamental concern is that the dual probe operates inside
a substrate we designed to host it. The independent resource
dynamics (Section~\ref{sec:method:substrate}) rule out the most
degenerate failure mode---AGR collapsing into a JSON-token
echo---but do not address whether the dissociation generalises
beyond the colony-simulation domain. A stronger validation would
re-instantiate the dual probe in a substrate we did not author---a
dialogue agent loop, a robotics planning loop, or an existing
structured-output agent benchmark instrumented post-hoc---and
confirm that the same probe definitions surface comparable
dissociation patterns. We expect generalisation to follow from the
structural property the substrate provides (a verbal channel and
an action channel sharing a single model state across cadences)
rather than the specific colony content, but the test is open. We
identify cross-substrate validation as the second priority for
follow-on work, alongside the broader matrix sweep; the present
findings should be read as conditional on it.
