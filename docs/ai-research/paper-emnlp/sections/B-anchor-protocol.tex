\section{Anchor Injection Protocol}
\label{app:anchors}

The five anchors injected at $t = 0$ in the pilot are summarised
in Table~\ref{tab:anchors}. Each anchor pairs a single-keyword
verbal token---chosen short and concrete to maximise the chance of
verbatim re-emission---with an implicit goal whose action key is
addressable on the substrate's action surface.

\begin{table}[h]
  \centering
  \footnotesize
  \begin{tabular}{l l}
    \toprule
    Verbal token & Implicit-goal action key \\
    \midrule
    \texttt{warehouse} & \texttt{deliver} \\
    \texttt{food}      & \texttt{farm} \\
    \texttt{wood}      & \texttt{wood} \\
    \texttt{guard}     & \texttt{guard\_engage} \\
    \texttt{quarry}    & \texttt{quarry} \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  \caption{Anchor pairs injected at $t = 0$ for the pilot.}
  \label{tab:anchors}
\end{table}

The on-disk anchor representation is JSON of the form

\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
\{ "verbal\_token": "warehouse",\\
\hspace*{1em} "implicit\_goal": \{\\
\hspace*{2em} "action": "deliver",\\
\hspace*{2em} "target": "warehouse"\\
\hspace*{1em} \},\\
\hspace*{1em} "anchor\_pos": [12, 8] \}
\end{quote}

\noindent
written into the LLM's persistent world-summary memory at $t = 0$
without further instruction. The substrate does not surface the
anchor pair to the model as a directive; the model must autonomously
integrate the pair through its own summarisation and policy-weight
behaviour.

\paragraph{Calibration of $|A| = 5$.}
The anchor count was calibrated during substrate development. With
$|A| = 3$, AFR saturated at $1.0$ for every model family at the
short horizon, eliminating discriminative power in the verbal
channel. With $|A| = 7$, the structured-output validity rate of
the smallest open-weight model dropped below 80\%, contaminating
both probes with parsing-driven zeros. $|A| = 5$ preserved
discrimination in the verbal probe while maintaining structured
output integrity across all three families we tested.
